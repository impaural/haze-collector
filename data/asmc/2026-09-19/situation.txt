Dry conditions persisted over most of the southern ASEAN region today, apart from parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Widespread hotspots were detected over Kalimantan while few to isolated hotspots were detected in Sumatra and Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspot clusters detected in parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Transboundary moderate smoke plumes from Kalimantan were observed drifting into the western parts of Sarawak and the southern parts of Sabah. Smoke haze from southern Sumatra and Kalimantan was also observed to drift towards Singapore and southern Peninsular Malaysia. Air quality stations in Kalimantan, the western parts of Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The overall hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. Over the northern ASEAN region, showers were observed and the hotspot and smoke haze situation remained subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, the southern ASEAN region is expected to remain dry, except for showers forecast over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo. While the showers may bring some relief to the hotspot and smoke haze situation, there is still a risk of transboundary haze occurrence especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow generally from the southeast to south. Wet weather is expected over the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot activity low. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to turn variable in direction, apart from the Philippines where the winds are likely to blow from the west.
