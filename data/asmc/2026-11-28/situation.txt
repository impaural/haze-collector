Wet weather conditions prevailed over most parts of the ASEAN region today, although drier weather persisted over parts of the Mekong sub-region and the Philippines. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in Kalimantan, Myanmar and Sulawesi. While moderate smoke haze was observed over the southern parts of Papua, the full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Most parts of the ASEAN region are likely to experience showers in the next few days, except for the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region where drier conditions are expected. Isolated hotspots and occasional smoke plumes may occur if dry weather is prolonged over fire-prone regions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the northeast to east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN are expected to be light and variable in direction.
