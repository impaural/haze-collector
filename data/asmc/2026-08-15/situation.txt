Most of the southern ASEAN region stayed dry today, apart from the northern parts of Sumatra, Peninsular Malaysia, and the northern parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Scattered hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and Sumatra, while few hotspots were detected in Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspot clusters detected in West Kalimantan and drift northwards into western Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed emanating from hotspots detected in large parts of Kalimantan, while slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspots detected in the central and southern parts of Sumatra. Several air quality stations in the northern and western parts of Borneo reported Unhealthy levels. The hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is forecast to continue over the southern ASEAN region, except over Peninsular Malaysia, parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where some showers are expected. Under prolonged dry conditions, the hotspot and smoke haze situation may remain escalated over fire-prone areas, with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the southeast to south. The northern ASEAN region is forecast to remain wet, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region expected to blow from the southwest to west.
