Dry conditions continued over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except for some showers over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, and the northern parts of Borneo. Clusters of scattered to widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and the southern and central parts of Sumatra. Few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed in other parts of Kalimantan, and the central and southern parts of Sumatra, while slight to moderate localised smoke plumes were observed in the eastern parts of Java. Several air quality stations in West Kalimantan, the western parts of Sarawak and Peninsular Malaysia, and the southern and central parts of Sumatra recorded Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Wet and cloudy conditions prevailed over the northern ASEAN region. A few hotspots were detected mainly in Viet Nam, but no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are forecast over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, apart from parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra, and the northeastern parts of Borneo, where showers are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to stay elevated in fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the southeast to southwest. Meanwhile, showers are forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region. The hotspot and smoke haze situation over the region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
