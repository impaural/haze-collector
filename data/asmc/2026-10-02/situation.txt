Dry conditions were observed over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today except for showers over parts of Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia as well as the northern parts of Borneo. Clusters of scattered hotspots were detected in the southern parts of Sumatra and in the southwestern parts of Kalimantan. Isolated hotspots were also detected in the eastern parts of Kalimantan. Transboundary moderate to dense smoke haze was observed in parts of Kalimantan, Sarawak, Singapore, the southern and central parts of Sumatra and the southern parts of Peninsular Malaysia. Air quality stations in parts of Kalimantan and Sumatra are reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous conditions. The hotspot and smoke haze situation could not be fully determined due to cloud cover over the region. For the northern ASEAN region, the hotspot and haze situation remain subdued and wet weather was observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is forecast to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region except for showers forecast over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra as well as the northern parts of Borneo. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve in areas encountering wet weather, however the situation can remain elevated in areas still facing prolonged dry conditions, leading to a persistent risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to continue blowing from the southeast or southwest. Meanwhile, over the northern ASEAN region, widespread wet weather is expected except for the central parts of Viet Nam where conditions are forecast to be relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to remain suppressed. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest or west.
