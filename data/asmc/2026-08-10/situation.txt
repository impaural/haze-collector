Dry conditions prevailed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected mostly in the southern parts of Sumatra and West Kalimantan, while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspots clusters detected in West Kalimantan drifting northeastwards into western Sarawak, as well as from the central parts of Sarawak drifting in the southeast direction into North Kalimantan. Moderate to dense localised smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspots detected in large parts of Kalimantan, the central parts of Sarawak, the eastern parts of Java, as well as the central and southern parts of Sumatra. A few air quality stations in West Kalimantan, the southern parts Sumatra, and the western and central parts of Sarawak recorded Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry conditions are expected over most parts of the southern ASEAN region, except over the northern and central parts of Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia where some showers are forecast. The hotspots and smoke haze situation may escalate in areas experiencing pro-longed periods of dry weather conditions, with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast to south. On the other hand, wet weather is expected to persist over the northern ASEAN region, which helps to keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected be blow from the southwest to west.
