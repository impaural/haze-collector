Wet weather prevailed over the northern ASEAN region today, while drier conditions were observed over parts of Borneo, Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, and the southern parts of Sumatra. Few to isolated hotspots were detected mainly in West and South Kalimantan, the southern parts of Sumatra, and Java. Moderate smoke plumes were observed emanating from hotspots in West Kalimantan, drifting in the northwest direction into Sarawak. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were also observed in parts of Kalimantan and the eastern parts of Java.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, showers are forecast over many parts of the ASEAN region, except for drier conditions over Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, the southern parts of Sumatra, and the southern and central parts of Kalimantan. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may escalate over fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the east or southeast. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest to west, except over the Philippines where winds are expected to blow mainly from the west to northwest.
