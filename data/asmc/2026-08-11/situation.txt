Dry conditions were observed over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today except for showers observed over parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the northern parts of Sumatra. Clusters of scattered hotspots were detected in West Kalimantan while a few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in Borneo. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in the southern parts of Sumatra and a few hotspots were detected in Java and Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in many parts of Kalimantan, and were observed to drift northwards from West Kalimantan into the western parts of Sarawak. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were also observed in the southern parts of Sumatra and the eastern parts of Java.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Dry weather is forecast to continue over most parts of the southern ASEAN region in the coming days except for the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and Sumatra where some showers may occur. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may remain escalated in areas facing prolonged dry conditions, with increased chances of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or south. Meanwhile, over the northern ASEAN region, widespread wet weather is expected except for relatively drier conditions over the central parts of Viet Nam. The hotspot and haze situation is expected to remain suppressed in the coming days. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region is expected to blow from the southwest.
