The southern ASEAN region remained dry today except for parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra, and the central parts of Borneo where there were showers. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected over Kalimantan and Sumatra. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed over the northern parts of Borneo. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed over other parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Some air quality stations in Kalimantan, Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The overall hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. Wet weather and cloudy conditions were observed over the northern ASEAN region and the hotspot activity stayed low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is expected to persist over the southern ASEAN region, except over Peninsular Malaysia as well as the northern and central parts of Sumatra and Borneo where some showers are forecast. The showers may bring some relief to the hotspot and smoke haze situation, but there remains a risk of severe transboundary haze occurrence especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow mainly from the southeast to south. The northern ASEAN region is expected to remain wet, which should keep the hotspot activity low. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be variable in direction, apart from the Philippines where the winds are likely to blow from the north to northeast.
