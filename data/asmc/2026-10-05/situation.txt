Dry weather was observed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today except for showers over the northern parts of Sumatra and Kalimantan, as well as over parts of Sumatra. Clusters of scattered hotspots were detected in the southern parts of Kalimantan. Isolated hotspots were detected in southern Sumatra and Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke plumes in the southern parts of Sumatra were observed to drift north into parts of Peninsular Malaysia and Singapore. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed in parts of Kalimantan. Some air quality stations in Singapore, the southern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and parts of Sumatra are reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. Air quality stations in parts of Kalimantan are also reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The hotspot situation could not be fully determined due to obscuration by thick smoke haze. Meanwhile, scattered showers were observed throughout the northern ASEAN region. Isolated hotspots were detected in parts of Viet Nam. Slight localised smoke plumes were observed in the central parts of Viet Nam.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry weather is expected to continue over most parts of the southern ASEAN region except for showers forecast over the northern parts of Sumatra, Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia. Under these dry conditions, the hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to remain escalated with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or southwest. For the northern ASEAN region, wet weather is forecast to continue, and the hotspot and haze situation is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region is forecast to be variable in direction.
