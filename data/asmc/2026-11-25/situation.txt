Showers were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except over the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected mainly in the southern parts of Sumatra and the Mekong sub-region. Slight to moderate localised smoke plumes were observed to emanate from a few hotspots in the southern parts of Myanmar and Viet Nam, as well as the central parts of Thailand. Moderate haze was also observed in the southern parts of Papua. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, showers are expected over many parts of the ASEAN region, except over the central and northern parts of Mekong sub-region where it is forecast to be relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain suppressed, but isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may continue to develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the northeast or east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the northwest or south.
