Wet weather conditions were observed over much of the ASEAN region today, except over most parts of the Mekong sub-region and southern parts of Kalimantan where drier conditions prevailed. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in the Mekong sub-region, Kalimantan and Sulawesi. While no significant smoke plumes were observed from satellite imagery, the full extent of the hotspot and haze situation in the region could not be ascertained due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, wet weather is likely to persist over much of the ASEAN region except over the Mekong sub-region. Localised hotspots and smoke plumes may still develop over drier and fire-prone areas. The prevailing winds are forecast to blow from the northeast or east over areas north of the Equator, while the prevailing winds over areas south of the Equator are expected to blow from the southeast.
