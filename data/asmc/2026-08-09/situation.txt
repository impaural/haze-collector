Dry weather continued over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except for isolated showers over the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia. Clusters of scattered to widespread hotspots were detected in West Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra, while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed to emanate from hotspots detected in other parts of Kalimantan, the central parts of Sarawak, and the central and southern parts of Sumatra. A few air quality stations in West Kalimantan, and the central and western parts of Sarawak recorded Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are forecast to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, except over the northern and central parts of Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia, and parts of North Kalimantan where showers are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may escalate in fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the southeast or south. Meanwhile, showers are forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region in the coming days. The hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest or west.
