Dry weather was observed over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today, with the exception of showers observed over parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the northern parts of Sumatra. Widespread hotspots were detected in West, Central and South Kalimantan. Isolated hotspots were detected in other parts of Kalimantan and the Lesser Sunda Isalnds. A few hotspots were detected in Sumatra, Java and Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in West, Central and South Kalimantan, with moderate smoke plumes from West Kalimantan observed to drift northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate smoke plumes were also observed in North Kalimantan and the southern and central parts of Sumatra. For the northern ASEAN region, widespread showers were observed today. The hotspot count remained low and no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Dry conditions are forecast to prevail over the southern parts of Sumatra, the central and southern parts of Borneo, the eastern parts of Java, as well as parts of Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands. Showers are forecast over the rest of the southern ASEAN region. In areas facing prolonged dry conditions, the hotspot and haze situation may remain escalated with increased chances of transboundary haze. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or southwest. For the northern ASEAN region, wet weather conditions are forecast to continue for the next few days. The hotspot and haze situation over the region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
