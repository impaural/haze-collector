Dry weather persisted over the southern ASEAN region today, except for some showers over parts of northern Sumatra, northern Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia. Widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and Sumatra, while few hotspots were detected in Sarawak. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed over Peninsular Malaysia, parts of Kalimantan, Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra, the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed over Papua and Sulawesi. Air quality stations in Kalimantan, Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, Sumatra and the western parts of Sarawak reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Showers prevailed over the northern ASEAN region, apart from the northeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. Few hotspots were detected in Viet Nam and Lao PDR, but no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is expected to persist over the southern ASEAN region, apart from Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Sumatra and parts of Borneo where showers are forecast. While showers may bring some respite, the hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain elevated especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a continued risk of severe transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow generally from the southeast to south, except over the northern parts of Borneo where the winds are forecast to be light and variable in direction. Wet weather is likely to continue over the northern ASEAN region, apart from the northeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where it is expected to be relatively drier. Hotspot activity is forecast to stay low. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the east to southeast, except over the northern and western parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are expected to be light and variable in direction.
