Showers and cloudy conditions were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except over the central parts of Mekong sub-region, and parts of Sumatra and Borneo where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were mostly detected in the southern parts of Myanmar and Sumatra while few hotspots were detected in Thailand, Cambodia, Kalimantan and Java. Moderate smoke plumes were observed in the central parts of Sulawesi and southwest from Papua, while slight localised smoke plumes observed to emanate from a few hotspots detected in southern Sumatra and Java. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: For the next few days, showers and cloudy conditions are expected over the ASEAN region with the exception of southern Sumatra, Java and the central Mekong sub-region where conditions are relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain subdued. Isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still develop in areas facing drier conditions. Over the northern ASEAN region, the prevailing winds are forecast to blow from the northeast to east, while over the southern ASEAN region, winds are forecast to blow from the east to southeast.
