Dry conditions were observed over parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over Java, Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Sumatra, and parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Scattered hotspots were detected in East Kalimantan, while few hotspots were detected in southern Sumatra and the central parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed over the parts of Kalimantan, the southern parts of Sumatra, the southeastern parts of Sulawesi and the southern parts of Papua. Few air quality stations in southern Kalimantan and Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot situation over Kalimantan and Sumatra could not be determined due to obscuration by thick smoke haze and cloud cover, while the smoke haze situation over parts of the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. The northern ASEAN region experienced dry conditions. Few hotspots were detected in the Mekong sub-region, but no significant smoke plumes were observed from satellite imagery.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is expected over parts of the southern ASEAN region, apart from Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra, Java and parts of Borneo where showers are forecast. While showers may bring some improvement, the hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain elevated over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow generally from the east to southeast, except over Borneo where the winds are expected to be variable in direction. Showers are forecast over the northern ASEAN region, except for relatively drier conditions over the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region. Isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may develop over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the northeast to east, apart from the northern parts of Mekong sub-region where the winds are expected to be light and variable in direction.
