Dry weather continued over most parts of the southern ASEAN region overnight, except for some isolated showers mainly over the northeastern parts of Borneo and northern parts of Sumatra. Widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan, while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspot clusters in many parts of Kalimantan, while slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed in the central and southern parts of Sumatra. Several air quality stations in West and Central Kalimantan, the western and central parts of Sarawak, and the southern parts of Sumatra recorded Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Wet weather prevailed over the northern ASEAN region overnight. Hotspot and smoke haze activities remained subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are forecast to continue over most of the southern ASEAN region, apart from the western coastal and northern parts of Sumatra, the northern parts of Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia, where showers are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may continue to escalate in fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the southeast to southwest. Meanwhile, wet weather is expected to continue over the northern ASEAN region, where the hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest to west.
