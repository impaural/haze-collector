Scattered hotspots were detected in many parts of Kalimantan while isolated hotspots were detected in Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, Sulawesi and the southern parts of Sumatra. Moderate smoke plumes were observed in the southern and western parts of Kalimantan. Slight localised smoke plumes were also observed in the southern and central parts of Sumatra. Extensive cloud cover prevent full observation of the current hotspot and smoke haze situation.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Dry conditions are expected to continue over the southern parts of Borneo, Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands and Sulawesi while a few showers are forecast over the rest of the ASEAN region. While showers may bring some respite to the hotspot and haze situation in some areas, other areas facing prolonged dry conditions may still experience escalated activities with risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the west or south. Wet weather is expected to continue over the northern ASEAN region except for the central parts of the Mekong sub-region and parts of Viet Nam where dry weather is forecast. The hotspot and haze situation is expected to remain subdued over the region. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest.
