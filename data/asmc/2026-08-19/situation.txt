Dry weather was observed over most of the southern ASEAN region today, apart from showers over the northern and central parts of Sumatra, and parts of Peninsular Malaysia. Widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan, while few to isolated hotspots were detected in Sulawesi, Java and Sumatra. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed elsewhere in Kalimantan and in the central parts of Sarawak, while slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed in the southern and central parts of Sumatra. Several air quality stations in West and Central Kalimantan, and the western parts of Sarawak recorded Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Meanwhile, wet weather was observed over the northern ASEAN region. Hotspot and smoke haze activities remained subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are forecast over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, except over the western coastal parts of Sumatra, parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the western parts of Java, and the northeastern parts of Borneo, where showers are likely. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may continue to escalate in fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the southeast to southwest. On the other hand, wet weather is expected to continue over the northern ASEAN region in the coming days. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to remain subdued over the region. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
