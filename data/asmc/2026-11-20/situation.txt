Showers prevailed over most of the AEAN region, apart from parts of the Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions were observed. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in the Mekong sub-region, Peninsular Malaysia and Kalimantan. Slight localised smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspots detected in the southern parts of Myanmar. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, wet weather conditions are expected over the ASEAN region, except over the central parts of the Mekong sub-region where it is likely to be relatively drier. The overall hotspot and smoke haze situation is forecast to remain suppressed, although isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still occur over drier areas. The prevailing winds over areas north of the Equator are expected to blow from the northeast to east, apart from the northern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are likely to be light and variable in direction. The prevailing winds over areas south of the Equator are forecast to be mainly light and variable in direction.
