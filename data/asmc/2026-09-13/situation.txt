The southern ASEAN region remained dry today, except over the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and southern parts of Sumatra and the western parts of Sarawak where there were some showers. Widespread hotspots were detected over Kalimantan while few to isolated hotspots were detected in Sumatra and Sarawak. Transboundary smoke haze was observed drifting from the northern parts of Kalimantan into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspots detected in other parts of Kalimantan as well as the central and southern parts of Sumatra. Air quality stations in Central and West Kalimantan, the western parts of Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. Showers were observed over the northern ASEAN region today and the hotspot and smoke haze situation remained subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, most of the southern ASEAN region is forecast to remain dry, except for some showers expected over parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the northern parts of Sumatra and Borneo. Though the showers may bring some relief to the hotspot and smoke haze situation, there is still a risk of transboundary haze occurrence especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the southeast to south. Wet weather is forecast over the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot activity low. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the southwest to west, apart from the Philippines and the northeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are likely to blow from the northeast to east.
