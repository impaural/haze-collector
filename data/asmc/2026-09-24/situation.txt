Dry conditions persisted over the southern ASEAN region today, except over Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and Sumatra. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed to cover the northwestern parts of Borneo. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed over other parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Air quality stations in Kalimantan, Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The overall hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. The northern ASEAN region stayed wet, and the hotspot activity remained low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is forecast over the southern ASEAN region, apart from parts of Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra and Borneo where showers are expected. While showers may bring some relief to the hotspot and smoke haze situation, but there remains a risk of transboundary haze occurrence especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow mainly from the southeast to southwest. Showers are forecast over the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to be variable in direction.
