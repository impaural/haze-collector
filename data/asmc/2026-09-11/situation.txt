The southern ASEAN region remained dry today, except over Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Scattered hotspots were mostly detected over the southern and western parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed over parts of Kalimantan, Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra. Air quality stations in West Kalimantan, Sarawak and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Showers were observed over the northern ASEAN region and the hotspot activity stayed low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry weather is forecast to persist over the southern ASEAN region, except over Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where showers are expected. While the showers might bring some relief to the overall hotspot and smoke haze situation, there still remains a risk of transboundary haze occurrence, particularly over fire-prone areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the southeast to south. On the other hand, wet weather is forecast over the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the northeast to north, apart from the southeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are likely to blow from the southwest.
