Wet weather and cloudy conditions were observed over most parts of the ASEAN region, except the Mekong sub-region, some parts of Borneo, the Philippines and Sulawesi where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in the Mekong sub-region, Kalimantan and Sulawesi. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, the ASEAN region is forecast to remain wet, apart from the northern and central Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are expected. While the overall hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain subdued, isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still occur over drier areas. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the northeast to east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast be mainly light and variable in direction.
