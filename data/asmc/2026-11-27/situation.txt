Wet weather and cloudy conditions were observed over the ASEAN region today, except over parts of the Mekong sub-region and the Philippines where it was relatively drier. Few hotspots were detected in the Mekong sub-region, Kalimantan and Sarawak. Slight localised smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspots detected in the southern parts of Myanmar. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed over the southern parts of Papua and the eastern parts of Sulawesi. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, showers are forecast over most of the ASEAN region, apart from the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are expected. While the overall hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to stay subdued, isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still occur over drier areas. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the north to northeast, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN are expected to be variable in direction.
