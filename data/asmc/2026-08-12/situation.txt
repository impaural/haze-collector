Dry conditions were observed over the southern ASEAN region today with the exception of showers over the northern parts of Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia. Clusters of scattered hotspots were observed in West Kalimantan while a few to isolated hotspots were detected in other parts of Kalimantan, Sarawak, the southern parts of Sumatra, the eastern parts of Java as well as Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in many parts of Kalimantan, Sarawak, as well as the central and southern parts of Sumatra. The hotspot and haze situation could not be fully determined due to cloud cover over the southern ASEAN region.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is expected to persist in most parts of the southern ASEAN region except for the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and Sumatra where some showers are forecast. Prolonged dry conditions can lead to an escalated hotspot and smoke haze situation with increased chances of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or south. Meanwhile, over the northern ASEAN region, widespread wet weather is expected except for relatively drier conditions over the central parts of Viet Nam and the southern parts of the Philippines. The hotspot and haze situation is forecast to remain subdued under the expected wet weather. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest.
