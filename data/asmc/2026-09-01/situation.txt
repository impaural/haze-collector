Dry weather persisted over most of the southern ASEAN region today, apart from the northeastern parts of Borneo, the northern parts of Sumatra and parts of Peninsular Malaysia where showers were observed. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected over Kalimantan and Sumatra while few hotspots were detected in Sarawak. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed to cover the northern parts of Borneo, as well as the southwestern, western and central parts of the Philippines. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspot clusters in other parts of Kalimantan and the southern and central parts of Sumatra. Air quality stations in the northern and western parts of Borneo and the southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. Meanwhile, showers were observed over the northern ASEAN region and the hotspot activity stayed low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, most of the southern ASEAN region is likely to remain dry, except over the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and Sumatra, as well as the northeastern parts of Borneo where some showers are forecast. Under prolonged dry conditions, the hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to stay elevated over fire-prone areas, with continued risk of severe transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the southeast to south. Wet weather is forecast over most of the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the southwest to west.
