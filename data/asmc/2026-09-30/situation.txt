Dry conditions were observed over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today except for showers over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, parts of Kalimantan as well as the northern and central parts of Sumatra. Isolated hotspots were detected in the southern parts of Sumatra and parts of Kalimantan. Transboundary moderate to dense smoke haze was observed in parts of Sarawak and Kalimantan. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed over Sumatra. Air quality stations in parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra are reporting Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy conditions. The hotspot and smoke haze situation could not be fully determined due to cloud cover over the region. For the northern ASEAN region, wet weather was observed. Isolated hotspots were detected in the central parts of Viet Nam where slight localized smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is forecast to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region except for showers forecast over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra as well as the northwestern parts of Borneo. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve in areas encountering wet weather, however the situation can remain elevated in areas still facing prolonged dry conditions, leading to a persistent risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to continue blowing from the east or southeast except for areas close to the Equator where the prevailing winds are forecast to be light and variable in direction. Meanwhile, over the northern ASEAN region, widespread wet weather is expected except for the northern parts of Viet Nam where conditions may be relatively drier. The hotspot and haze situation is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be variable in direction.
