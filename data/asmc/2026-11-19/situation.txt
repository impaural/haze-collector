Most of the ASEAN region experienced wet weather and cloudy conditions today, apart from parts of the Mekong sub-region and the Philippines where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in the Mekong sub-region and Kalimantan. Slight localised smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspots detected in the southern parts of Viet Nam. Moderate smoke haze was also observed over the southern parts of Papua. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, wet weather is forecast to continue over the ASEAN region, except over the parts of northern and central Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are expected. While the overall hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to stay subdued, isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still occur over drier areas. The prevailing winds over areas north of the Equator are forecast to blow from the northeast to east, apart from the northern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are expected to be light and variable in direction. The prevailing winds over areas south of the Equator are likely to be variable in direction.
