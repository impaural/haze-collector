The southern ASEAN region remained dry today, except for some showers over parts of Peninsular Malaysia, as well as northern and central Sumatra. Scattered hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra, while few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed over parts of Kalimantan, Singapore, Sarawak, the southern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the central and southern parts of Sumatra. Smoke haze was also observed drifting from Kalimantan into Sabah and the southern parts of the Philippines. Air quality stations in parts of Borneo, Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, as well as the central and southern parts of Sumatra reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Showers were observed over the northern ASEAN region, apart from the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. The hotspot activity over the northern ASEAN region remained low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry weather is expected to persist over parts of the southern ASEAN region, except over Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where showers are forecast. While showers may bring some improvement to the hotspot and smoke haze situation, there is still a risk of transboundary haze occurrence especially over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the southeast to southwest. Wet weather is expected to continue over the northern ASEAN region, apart from parts of northern Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are forecast. The hotspot activity is likely to remain low. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the northeast to east, except over the northwestern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are forecast to be light and variable in direction.
