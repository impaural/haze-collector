Dry conditions were observed over the southern ASEAN region today except for some showers in the northern parts of Borneo and Sumatra and over parts of Peninsular Malaysia. A cluster of scattered hotspots were detected in South Kalimantan. Isolated hotspots were detected in the central and southern parts of Sumatra, as well as in other parts of Kalimantan and Sulawesi. A few hotspots were detected in Java. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in parts of Kalimantan, Sumatra and Sulawesi. A smoke plume was observed in Johor, Malaysia. Air quality stations in West Kalimantan and the western parts of Sarawak are reporting Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy Levels. Air quality stations in Riau, Jambi, South Sumatra and the western parts of Peninsular Malaysia are reporting Unhealthy levels. The hotspot and haze situation could not be fully determined due to cloud cover over some parts of the region. - For the northern ASEAN region, wet weather conditions were observed with heavier showers observed in the northern parts of Viet Nam from Tropical Storm Narra. The hotspot and haze situation over the region was observed to be subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Dry conditions are forecast to continue over the southern ASEAN region over the next few days except for some showers over the northern parts of Borneo, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and over Peninsular Malaysia. The hotspot and haze situation in the region is expected to remain escalated with continued risk of transboundary haze. The prevailing winds in the region are forecast to blow from the southeast or south. - Showers are forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region over the next few days, which can help to suppress the hotspot and haze situation over the region. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
