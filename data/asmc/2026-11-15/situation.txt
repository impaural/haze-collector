Wet weather and cloudy conditions were observed over most parts of the ASEAN region, except the Mekong sub-region, some parts of Borneo, the Philippines, Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in Myanmar, Thailand, Viet Nam and Kalimantan. However, no significant smoke plumes were observed due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the next few days, wet weather is expected over most of the ASEAN region, except over the Mekong sub-region and the Philippines. While the overall hotspot activity and smoke haze situation is likely to remain subdued, hotspots with localized smoke plumes can occur over areas which are drier. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be light and variable in direction, except over the Philippines and southern parts of Viet Nam where the winds are expected to blow from the northeast to east. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected be blow from the east to southeast.
