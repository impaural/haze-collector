Dry weather prevailed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over the northern parts of Borneo where isolated showers were observed. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected mainly in Sumatra and Kalimantan, while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate transboundary smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspots in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed emitting from hotspot clusters in most parts of Kalimantan, as well as in central and southern Sumatra. A few air quality stations in West Kalimantan recorded Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are expected over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, except for some isolated showers over the northern and central parts of Sumatra, the northeastern parts of Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia. Hotspot and smoke haze activities may continue to escalate in fire-prone region experiencing prolonged dry weather, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or southwest. Meanwhile, showers are forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region in the coming days, which helps to keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region subdued. The prevailing winds over the region are expected to blow mainly from the southwest to west.
