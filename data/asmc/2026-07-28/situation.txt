Showers were observed over the ASEAN region today while drier conditions were observed in parts of Sumatra, Borneo, Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands. Isolated hotspots were detected in West Kalimantan, while a few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the Kalimantan region. A few hotspots were also detected in central Viet Nam, the southern parts of Sumatra as well as Java and Sulawesi. Moderate smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspots in West Kalimantan and drift northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. The full hotspot and haze situation could not be determined due to extensive cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, wet weather is forecast to continue over most parts of the ASEAN region, with isolated showers forecast over Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, the western parts of Borneo and the southern parts of Sumatra. Despite the presence of these showers, isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes can still emerge in some drier areas. The prevailing winds in the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest to west. The prevailing winds in the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the east to southeast.
