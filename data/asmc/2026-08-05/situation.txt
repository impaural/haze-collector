Dry weather prevailed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over some parts of northern Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia where isolated showers were observed. Scattered hotspots were detected mainly in Sumatra and Kalimantan. Transboundary smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspots in West Kalimantan, drifting northwards into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed emitting from hotspots in most parts of Kalimantan, as well as in southern Sumatra. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are expected over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, except for some isolated showers over the western coast of Sumatra and parts of Peninsular Malaysia. Hotspot and smoke haze activities may escalate in fire-prone region experiencing prolonged dry weather, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or south. Meanwhile, showers are forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region in the coming days, which helps to keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region subdued. The prevailing winds over the region are expected to blow mainly from the southwest to west.
