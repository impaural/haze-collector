Dry weather prevailed over parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over Sumatra, Peninsular Malaysia and some parts of Borneo where showers were observed. Scattered hotspots were detected mainly in the southern parts of Kalimantan while few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in southern and eastern Kalimantan, as well as the northern and central parts of Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed in the southern parts of Papua. A few air quality stations in Sumatra and Kalimantan are reporting Unhealthy levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to extensive cloud cover. It was dry over most parts of the northern ASEAN region, except for some showers over the southern and eastern parts of the Mekong sub-region. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in southern Myanmar and central Thailand, but no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry conditions are expected over parts of the southern ASEAN region, except Peninsular Malaysia, Java and parts of Sumatra and Borneo where showers are forecast. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve in areas experiencing showers but also continue to stay elevated in areas experiencing prolonged periods of dry weather, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast to southwest. On the other hand, wet weather is expected to persist over the northern ASEAN region, except for the northern parts of the Mekong sub-region where it is forecast to be relatively drier. Isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be northeast or east.
