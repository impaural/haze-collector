Widespread showers were observed over the ASEAN region today while drier conditions were observed in the southern parts of Sumatra, Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands. A few hotspots were detected in the northern and southern parts of Sumatra, the eastern and western parts of Kalimantan as well as in parts of Java and Sulawesi. Localised smoke plumes were observed in the southern parts of Sumatra as well as the eastern and western parts of Kalimantan. The full hotspot and haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be determined due to extensive cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Wet weather is expected to prevail over the ASEAN region in the coming days. Dry conditions is forecast over the southern parts of Sumatra and Kalimantan, as well as over parts of Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands. These dry conditions can lead to the emergence of isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes. The prevailing winds over areas north of the Equator are forecast to from the southwest or west. The prevailing winds over areas south of the Equator are forecast to blow from the east. The winds over the Philippines are forecast to be light and variable in direction.
