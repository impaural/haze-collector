It was dry over Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, parts of Borneo, and the central and southern parts of Sumatra. Showers were observed elsewhere in the ASEAN region. Scattered hotspots were detected in Kalimantan, while few to isolated hotspots were detected in Java, Sumatra, Sabah, Sarawak, Thailand and Viet Nam. A moderate smoke plume was observed drifting in the northwest direction from the northern parts of Kalimantan into the eastern parts of Sarawak. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspots detected in parts of Kalimantan and the eastern parts of Java.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry weather is expected over parts of Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, Kalimantan, and the southern parts of Sumatra, while wet weather is forecast elsewhere in the ASEAN region. The hotspot and some haze situation may escalate over areas under prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the east to south. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the southwest to west.
