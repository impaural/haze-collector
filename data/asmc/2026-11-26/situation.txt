Showers were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except over the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and the Mekong sub-region. Moderate to dense haze was observed in the southern parts of Papua. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, wet weather is expected over many parts of the ASEAN region, except over the central and northern parts of Mekong sub-region where it is forecast to be relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain suppressed, but isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may continue to develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the northeast or east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the northwest or southeast.
