Dry conditions were observed over most of the southern ASEAN region today. Isolated hotspots were detected in West Kalimantan and the southern and central parts of Sumatra. A few hotspots were detected other parts of Kalimantan and Sumatra, as well as in Java and Peninsular Malaysia. Moderate to dense smoke plumes from Kalimantan were observed to generally drift north into parts of Sarawak, Sabah and Brunei. Moderate to dense plumes from the southern and central Sumatra, Singapore and the southern parts of Peninsular Malaysia. Many air quality stations in Borneo are reporting Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy levels. Some of the air quality stations in Peninsular Malaysia and Sumatra are reporting Moderate to Unhealthy levels. The hotspot counts may not be fully represented due to obscuration from dense smoke haze. The haze situation could not be fully determined due to cloud cover over parts of the region. - For the northern ASEAN region, widespread wet weather was observed. A few hotspots were detected in Thailand and Viet Nam but no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, showers are forecast over parts of Borneo and the northern parts of Sumatra and Peninsular Malaysia. Dry conditions are expected to continue over the rest of the southern ASEAN region. Escalated hotspot and smoke haze activities with a risk of severe transboundary haze occurrences occur over areas with expected dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the region are expected to blow from the southeast or south. - Meanwhile, wet weather is forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region. The hotspot and haze situation in this region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern region are expected to blow from the southwest or west.
