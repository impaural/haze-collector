Wet weather and cloudy conditions were observed over the ASEAN region today, except over the Philippines, the eastern parts of Borneo and the southern and eastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. Isolated to scattered hotspots were detected mainly in the eastern parts of Kalimantan and the central parts of Viet Nam. Slight to moderate localised smoke plumes were observed emitting from hotspots detected in the eastern parts of Kalimantan and the central parts of Viet Nam. Moderate smoke haze was also observed over the southern parts of Papua. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, the ASEAN region is forecast to remain wet, apart from the northwestern and southeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are expected. While the overall hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain subdued, isolated hotspots and localised smoke plumes may still occur over drier areas. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region and the northwestern parts of the Mekong sub-region are forecast to be light and variable in direction, while the prevailing winds over other parts of the northern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the northeast to east.
