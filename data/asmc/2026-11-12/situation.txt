Showers were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region, and some parts of Borneo where it was relatively drier. Isolated hotspots were detected in the Kalimantan. Moderate smoke plumes were observed to emanate from a few hotspots in the eastern parts of Kalimantan. Moderate smoke haze was observed in the central parts of Sulawesi and the southern parts of Papua. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, showers are expected over many parts of the ASEAN region, except over the central and northern parts of Mekong sub-region where it is forecast to be relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain suppressed, but isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may continue to develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be northeast or east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the east or southeast.
