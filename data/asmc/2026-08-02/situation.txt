Dry conditions prevailed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Borneo and the northern and central parts of Sumatra, where isolated showers were observed. Scattered hotspots were detected mainly in Kalimantan, while a few to isolated hotspots were mostly detected in Java, the Lesser Sunda islands, Sulawesi and the southern and central parts of Sumatra. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed in parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Smoke plumes from West Kalimantan were observed to drift northwards and into the western parts of Sarawak. In the northern ASEAN region, showers and cloudy conditions prevailed. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in Viet Nam, with slight smoke plumes observed to emit from hotspots detected in the southern parts of Viet Nam. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation over the region could not be determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather conditions are forecast to continue over most of the southern ASEAN region, except for Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Borneo, and the northern and central parts of Sumatra where showers are expected. In the northern ASEAN region, showers are expected to persist. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may escalate in areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the east or south, while the prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southwest or west.
