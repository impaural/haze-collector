Dry weather prevailed over most of the southern ASEAN region today, except over parts of northern Peninsular Malaysia, parts of northern and central Sumatra, and the western parts of Borneo where some showers were observed. Scattered hotspots were detected in Kalimantan, while few hotspots were detected in Sumatra, Sabah and Sarawak. Moderate smoke haze was observed over the southeastern and the northwestern parts of Kalimantan. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspots detected in other parts of Kalimantan, the southern parts of Sumatra and the eastern parts of Sarawak. A few air quality stations in the western parts of Borneo reported Unhealthy levels. The hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to extensive cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, the southern ASEAN region is forecast to remain dry, apart from the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra and parts of Borneo where some showers are expected. The hotspot and some haze situation may continue to escalate over fire- prone areas under prolonged dry conditions, with risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow from the southeast to south. Scattered showers are forecast over most of the northern ASEAN region, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region expected to blow mainly from the southwest.
