Dry weather prevailed over parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except over Sumatra, the northern parts of Peninsular Malaysia and parts of Borneo where showers were observed. Scattered hotspots were detected in the eastern parts of Kalimantan while few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate transboundary smoke haze was observed to cover the northwestern parts of Borneo. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed to emanate from hotspot clusters in East Kalimantan. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed in the central parts of Sulawesi and the southern parts of Papua. Several air quality stations in Borneo and Sumatra are reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to extensive cloud cover. It was dry over most parts of the northern ASEAN region, except for some showers over the central parts of the Mekong sub-region. Isolated hotspots were detected mainly in central Thailand and Viet Nam, with slight localised smoke plumes observed to emanate from a few hotspots in central Viet Nam.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry conditions are expected over parts of the southern ASEAN region, except over Sumatra, Peninsular Malaysia, Java and parts of Borneo where showers are forecast. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve in areas experiencing showers but also continue to stay elevated in areas experiencing prolonged periods of dry weather, with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the northeast to southeast. On the other hand, wet weather is expected over the northern ASEAN region. Isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may still develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be light and variable in direction.
