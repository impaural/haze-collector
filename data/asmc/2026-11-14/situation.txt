Showers were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except the Mekong sub-region, some parts of Borneo, Philippines and the Lesser Sunda Islands where drier conditions prevailed. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and Myanmar. No significant smoke plumes were observed from satellite due to cloudy conditions.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, showers are expected over many parts of the ASEAN region, except over the northern and central Mekong sub-region where drier conditions are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain suppressed, but isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may continue to develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be light and variable in direction, except over the Philippines where the winds are expected to blow from the northeast to east. The prevailing winds over southern ASEAN are forecast to blow from east to southeast.
