Showers were observed over many parts of the ASEAN region today, except the northern and central parts of the Mekong sub-region, and some parts of Borneo and Sumatra where it was relatively drier. Few to isolated hotspots were detected in the Kalimantan, Thailand, Lao PDR, Myanmar and Viet Nam. Slight to moderate smoke plumes were observed to emanate from a few hotspots in Myanmar and Viet Nam. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation in the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, showers are expected over many parts of the ASEAN region, except over the central and northern parts of Mekong sub-region where it is forecast to be relatively drier. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain suppressed, but isolated hotspots and smoke plumes may continue to develop in areas facing prolonged dry conditions. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be northeast or east, while the prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to be variable.
