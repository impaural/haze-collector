Dry conditions were observed over the southern ASEAN region today, except for a few showers observed in parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the northern parts of Borneo. Scattered hotspots were detected in parts of Kalimantan as well as the central and southern parts of Sumatra. A few hotspots were detected in parts of Peninsular Malaysia, Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands and Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed in many parts of Kalimantan. Moderate smoke plumes were observed to drift northwards from West Kalimantan into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate smoke plumes was also observed in the central and southern parts of Sumatra.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are expected to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region except for parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Borneo and the northern and central parts of Sumatra where some showers are forecast. Areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions may lead to an escalation in hotspot and smoke haze activities, with a risk of transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or south. Wet weather is forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region over the next few days. The hotspot and haze situation over the region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
