Dry weather continued over many parts of the southern ASEAN region today, except for showers mainly over the northern parts of Sumatra and the western parts of Sarawak. Widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra, while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed over Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, Kalimantan, Sumatra, and the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed over Papua and Sulawesi. Many air quality stations in Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, Kalimantan and Sumatra are reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot situation over Kalimantan and Sumatra could not be determined due to obscuration by thick smoke haze and cloud cover, while the smoke haze situation over parts of the region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. Showers were observed over the northern ASEAN region today, except over the northern parts of the Mekong sub-region where it was relatively drier. Few hotspots were detected in Viet Nam, Cambodia, and the northern parts of Thailand and Lao PDR, but no significant smoke plumes were observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry conditions are forecast to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region, apart from Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Sumatra, and parts of Borneo, where showers are expected. While the hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve in areas experiencing showers, the situation is expected to remain elevated in fire-prone regions experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a continued risk of severe transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are expected to blow mainly from the east-southeast to south. Meanwhile, showers are expected over the northern ASEAN region, except for relatively drier conditions forecast over the northeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast blow mainly from the northeast to east, except over the northwestern parts of the Mekong sub-region and southern parts of the Philippines where winds are expected to be light and variable in direction.
