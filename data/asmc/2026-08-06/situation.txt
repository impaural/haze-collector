Dry conditions were observed over most parts of the southern ASEAN today except for some showers observed parts of Peninsular Malaysia and the northern parts of Sumatra. Widespread hotspots were detected in West Kalimantan while isolated to scattered hotspots were detected elsewhere in Kalimantan. A few hotspots were also detected in parts of Peninsular Malaysia, Sarawak, Sumatra, Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands and Sulawesi. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed to drift northwards from West Kalimantan into the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were also observed in other parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Localised slight smoke plumes were also observed in the central parts of Sarawak.

Weather and Haze Outlook: In the coming days, dry conditions are expected to continue over many parts of the southern ASEAN region except for parts of Peninsular Malaysia, as well as the northern parts and the western coastal areas of Sumatra where some showers are forecast. Areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions may lead to an escalation in hotspot and smoke haze activities. Under the expected southeasterly or southerly prevailing winds over the region, there is a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrences. Wet weather is expected to persist over the northern ASEAN region. The hotspot and haze situation over the Mekong sub-region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southwest.
