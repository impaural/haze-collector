Dry conditions were observed over most of the southern ASEAN region today, except over the central parts of Peninsular Malaysia, the northern and central parts of Sumatra and the northeastern parts of Borneo where there were some showers. Scattered to widespread hotspots were detected over Kalimantan and Sumatra while few to isolated hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke plumes were observed to cover the West, Central and North Kalimantan, Sabah, Sarawak, Brunei and parts of Palawan, the Philippines. Moderate to dense smoke plumes were observed to emit from hotspot clusters in other parts of Kalimantan and the southern parts of Sumatra. Several air quality stations in Sarawak and West and Central Kalimantan reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be fully determined due to cloud cover. Wet weather was observed over the northern ASEAN region today, which kept the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry weather is forecast to persist over most of the southern ASEAN region, apart from Peninsular Malaysia, the northeastern parts of Borneo and the parts of northern and central Sumatra where some showers are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is likely to remain elevated especially over fire-prone areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with continued risk of severe transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast to south. Over the northern ASEAN region, showers are expected to prevail, which should keep the hotspot and smoke haze situation subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are likely to blow mainly from the southwest to west.
