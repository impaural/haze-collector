Dry conditions were observed over most parts of the southern ASEAN region today except for showers over most parts of Borneo. Clusters of scattered hotspots were detected in the central and southern parts of Sumatra. A few hotspots were detected along the southern coast of Kalimantan. Moderate to dense smoke haze was observed in the southern and central parts of Sumatra, many parts of Peninsular Malaysia, southern Viet Nam and the southwestern parts of Kalimantan. Many air quality stations in Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra, West Kalimantan and Central Kalimantan are reporting Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full hotspot and haze situation over Borneo could not be determined due to extensive cloud cover. Meanwhile, over the northern ASEAN region, a few hotspots were detected in the southern parts of Viet Nam, where a slight localized smoke plume was also observed.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Dry conditions are forecast to continue over the southern parts of Sumatra and most parts of Kalimantan, as well as over parts of Java and Sulawesi. Showers are forecast over the rest of the region, which may bring some respite to the hotspot and smoke haze situation. However, escalated fire activities can still occur in areas facing prolonged drier conditions, which can lead to transboundary haze occurrences. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow from the southeast or southwest. In the meantime, wet weather is forecast to continue over the northern ASEAN region. The hotspot and smoke haze situation over this region is expected to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are forecast to be light and variable in direction.
