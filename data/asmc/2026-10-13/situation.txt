Dry weather persisted over the southern ASEAN region today, except for some showers over parts of northern and central Sumatra, southern Peninsular Malaysia as well as northern and eastern Borneo. Widespread hotspots were detected in Kalimantan and Sumatra, while few hotspots were detected elsewhere in the southern ASEAN region. Moderate to dense transboundary smoke haze was observed over parts of Sumatra, Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, Kalimantan and the western parts of Sarawak. Moderate to dense smoke haze was also observed over Papua and Sulawesi. Air quality stations in Kalimantan, Peninsular Malaysia, Singapore, Sumatra and the western parts of Sarawak reported Unhealthy to Hazardous levels. The full extent of the hotspot and smoke haze situation over the southern ASEAN region could not be determined due to cloud cover. Showers continued over the northern ASEAN region, apart from the northern parts of the Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions were observed. Hotspot activity over the northern ASEAN region stayed low.

Weather and Haze Outlook: Over the next few days, dry conditions are likely to persist over the southern ASEAN region, apart from Peninsular Malaysia, the northern parts of Sumatra and the northern parts of Borneo where isolated showers are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation may improve over areas experiencing showers, but remain elevated over areas experiencing prolonged dry conditions, with a continued risk of transboundary haze occurrence. The prevailing winds over the southern ASEAN region are forecast to blow mainly from the southeast to south. Showers are likely to continue over the northern ASEAN region, except over the northeastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where relatively drier conditions are expected. The hotspot and smoke haze situation is forecast to remain subdued. The prevailing winds over the northern ASEAN region are likely to blow from the northeast to southeast, except over the northern and eastern parts of the Mekong sub-region where the winds are expected to be light and variable in direction.
